rLogin is Pyrelight's authentication plugin for Minecraft servers. It lets you run a server that accepts both premium (official Mojang/Microsoft account) players and non-premium (cracked) players, keeping everyone's account secure through smart, automatic login handling.

## What rLogin Does

When a player joins your server, rLogin automatically determines whether they are a premium or non-premium account and handles authentication accordingly:

- **Premium players** are verified against Mojang's session servers automatically — no password needed.
- **Non-premium players** register a password on their first join and use it to log in on every subsequent visit.

This means your players get a seamless experience regardless of whether they own the official game, and you don't have to run separate servers for each group.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Player Joins">
    A player connects to your server. rLogin intercepts the connection and checks whether their username corresponds to a premium Minecraft account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Premium Check">
    If the account is premium, rLogin performs Mojang session verification automatically. The player is logged in without any action required on their part.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Non-Premium Flow">
    If the account is non-premium, rLogin checks whether the player has previously registered. New players are prompted to register with `/register`; returning players are prompted to `/login`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Granted">
    Once authenticated, the player gains full access to your server. Until authentication is complete, player movement and commands are restricted.
  </Step>
</Steps>
